Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This is the Privacy Policy for the With Joyful Lips mobile application for Android and iOS, developed by Neil Jay (the "Developer"). It explains how the app handles information when you use it.

This policy covers the mobile app, not the separate With Joyful Lips Lyrics website or third-party applications and services that you choose to use. The website has its own privacy practices, including the use of Google Analytics, described in its [website privacy notice](https://withjoyfullipslyrics.com/about/#privacy).

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its features.

Hymns and categories are bundled with the app and are available offline. To provide its features, the app stores certain information locally on your device, including:

- Your favorite hymns.
- Your search history.
- Your theme and text-size preferences.
- Hymn-related data required for app functionality.

This information remains on your device. The app does not transmit your searches, favorites, reading activity, or locally stored hymn data to the Developer.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. The Developer does not sell your personal information and does not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 2. Device Backups

If you have enabled device or operating-system backups, your app data may be included in those backups.

Such backups are managed by your device's operating system or backup service provider and are subject to your device settings and the provider's applicable terms and privacy policy.

## 3. Feedback, Sharing, and Printing

### Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, it opens your default email application with a draft addressed to **ping [at] pm [dot] neiljay [dot] com**.

Depending on the information you provide, the draft may include:

- The subject and message you enter.
- Your selected feedback category.
- The app version.
- Your device platform (Android or iOS).
- Your app language.

The app does not automatically send this email. You can review, edit, and send the message yourself.

If your device has no email app that can handle the draft, the app copies it to your device's clipboard so you can paste it into another email app. The draft stays on your device until you delete it or copy something else.

If you send feedback, your email service provider and the Developer will process the information in your message to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

You may request the deletion of a feedback email by contacting the Developer at ping [at] pm [dot] neiljay [dot] com.

### Sharing and Printing

When you choose to share or print a hymn, the app passes the content you select to the sharing or printing application or service you choose.

Those third-party applications and service providers handle the information according to their own privacy policies and practices. The app does not receive information about the recipients with whom you share content.

### External Websites and Links

The app may open the With Joyful Lips Lyrics website or other external links in your browser.

When you visit an external website, your information is handled according to that website's own privacy policy. The app does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the app remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

You can clear your search history directly from the search screen.

If your device backs up app data, copies of that data may remain in your backups according to your device's backup settings and the backup provider's retention policies.

Feedback emails sent to the Developer are retained in the Developer's email account for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide support, and manage related requests.

If you have questions about a feedback message you submitted or wish to request its deletion, contact ping [at] pm [dot] neiljay [dot] com.

## 5. Data Security

The app stores its local data using the storage mechanisms provided by Android or iOS.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device. We recommend using an appropriate screen lock and reviewing your device's backup and security settings.

Information you choose to send through email, share through third-party applications, print using external services, or provide to external websites is handled by the respective service providers under their own security and privacy practices.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app does not require an account or knowingly collect personal information from children through its features.

If you have concerns about personal information submitted voluntarily through feedback or email, you may contact the Developer using the contact details below.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Because the app keeps information on your device and does not send it to the Developer, the Developer holds no personal information about you other than any feedback email you choose to send. Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply to you, you have the right to:

- Ask whether the Developer holds personal information about you and request a copy of it.
- Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
- Ask for information to be deleted, as described in Section 4.
- Object to or restrict how your information is used.
- Receive the information you provided in a portable format.
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

The Developer processes a feedback email only to respond to your inquiry and provide support. You can exercise these rights by contacting ping [at] pm [dot] neiljay [dot] com.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the app, its features, or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updates will be published through the appropriate app listing or another suitable channel. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.

You are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
