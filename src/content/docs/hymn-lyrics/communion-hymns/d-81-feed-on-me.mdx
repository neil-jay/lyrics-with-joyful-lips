## D.81 Feed on Me

***Chorus:***  
**Feed on me, and never will you hunger;**  
**Feed on me, and life will never end.**  

Do not work for bread that cannot fill you.  
Set your heart on food that long endures.  
I will feed the food you need,  
Take my words, my Father's life is yours.  

Moses brought you manna in the desert.  
Still you ask for signs from God on high.  
I'm the bread that God has fed,  
Believe in me, and you will never die.  

I will give my flesh as bread to feed you.  
I will give my blood as wine to share,  
Taste and know that love will grow,  
Dwell in me, you'll find my Father's care.
