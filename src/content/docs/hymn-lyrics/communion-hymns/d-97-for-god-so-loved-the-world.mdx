## D.97 For God so Loved the World

You shall love the Lord your God  
with all your heart,  
All your mind, all your strength.  
You shall love the Lord your God  
with all your heart,  
You shall love your neighbour as yourself.  

***Chorus:***  
**For God so loved the world.**  
**That He gave us his only Son.**  
**Whoever trusts in Him,**  
**Shall obtain eternal life. (2)**  

Tis by grace you have been saved  
through faith in Christ,  
Not by works, lest you boast.  
Even when your trespasses had rendered you dead,  
You were made alive in Jesus Christ.  

As the Lord has pardoned you,  
so must you forgive:  
Let his peace rule your heart,  
He has nailed your trespasses  
and sins to the cross.  
So give thanks to God through Jesus Christ.  

And I pray that Jesus Christ  
may dwell in your hearts,  
Through your faith, through your love.  
May his Spirit strengthen you  
with power and might.  
May your lives be rooted in love.
