## D.68 The Lord Jesus

***Chorus:***  
**The Lord Jesus, after eating with his friends,**  
**Washed their feet and said to them:**  
**"Do you know, what I, your Lord,**  
**have done to you?**  
**I have given you example**  
**That so you also should do".**  

You are my friends:  
No one can have a greater love  
Than to give one's life for one's friends.  

Peace I leave with you.  
My peace I give to all who live  
With sincere love for all mankind.  

I am the vine  
and you, the branch: remain in me  
And you will bear abundant fruit.  

You who come to me  
Will never thirst nor want for food  
And I will raise you up on the last day.
