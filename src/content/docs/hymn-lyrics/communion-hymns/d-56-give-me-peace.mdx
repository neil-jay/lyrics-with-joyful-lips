## D.56 Give Me Peace

Give me peace, O Lord, I pray,  
In my work and in my play.  
And inside my heart and mind,  
Lord, give me peace.  

To the world give peace, I pray,  
Let all quarrels cease to-day.  
May we spread your light and love,  
Lord, give us peace.
