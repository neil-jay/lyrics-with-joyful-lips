## D.110 Live on in My Love

***Chorus:***  
**Live on, live on in my love (2)**  
**As the Father has loved me, so I love you.**  
**Live on, live on in my love.**  

You will live in my love  
if you keep my commands.  
This commandment I give to you:  
That you love one another,  
each one as your brother (sister),  
Each one as I love you.  

I am the Vine, my Father the Vinegrower  
Who is looking for fruits of love.  
Barren branches He prunes off,  
fruitful branches  
He trims clean, trims clean of all selfishness.  

My Father will send you another Paraclete  
To be with you always, always.  
He's the Spirit of truth,  
He's the Spirit of peace, Spirit of unity.
