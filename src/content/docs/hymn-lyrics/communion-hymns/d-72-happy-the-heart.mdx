## D.72 Happy the Heart

***Chorus:***  
**Happy the heart that lives**  
**In the heart of God, the heart of love,**  
**Happy the heart that learns**  
**To love with the love of God.**  

God is love; Love your God  
With the love he gave in Christ.  

God loved us first, loved us best in Christ  
For there is no greater love.  

We must love, love every one  
With this love that comes from God.  

Love brings peace, peace brings hope.  
And hope never disappoints.  

Spirit of Love, live in us  
That our joy may be complete.
