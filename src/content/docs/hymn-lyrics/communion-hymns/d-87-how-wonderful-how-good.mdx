## D.87 How Wonderful, How Good

How wonderful, how good to live in Jesus.  
To let his love now fill  
Our minds and hearts and will.  
To rise with Jesus.  

***Chorus:***  
**So let this be our aim:**
**To make our lives proclaim**
**That God is love, that God is love.**

And since in unity we live together,  
Our quarrels now must cease  
And we must live at peace  
With Christ among us.  

This bread of life we share is Christ's own body.  
A sign that we must care  
For those who are in need  
The poor, the hungry.
