## D.125 I am the Living Bread

***Chorus:***  
**I am the living Bread that came from heaven**  
**And all who eat it will live for ever.**  
**The bread that I will give will be my own flesh**  
**And I will offer it to give you Life.**  

If you reject my flesh or fail to drink my blood  
I tell you solemnly, there'll be no life in you.  
If you do trust in me and do as I command,  
You'll have eternal life and live with me.  

My flesh is real food, my blood is real drink,  
He who does what I say will find my life in him,  
My Father gives me life when I do live in Him,  
That same life you will get, taking this bread.  

This is my sacrament which feeds my people.  
I grant them holiness and give them life divine  
So that they'll all be one, united in my love.  
I'll lead them with my hand to share my life.
