## D.112 Take, Eat and Hunger No Longer

***Chorus:***  
**Take, eat and hunger no longer,**  
**For I am the bread of life.**  
**Take, drink, this wine will refresh you,**  
**My cup overflows with love for you.**  

I am the living bread, broken for people.  
You are the vessels of silver and clay.  
Come and partake of the love that I offer.  
Then share it with others each day.  

Just as my Father has sent me to feed you,  
So must you now feed your neighbours in need.  
Don't be afraid for I'll always be with you.  
My spirit will guide you along.  

I lead you out of the power of darkness,  
I bring you safe to the kingdom of life.  
I set you free thru the Spirit I send you.  
Come forward to spread the good news.
