## D.11 Where Love and Charity

***Chorus:***  
**Where love and charity are found,**  
**God there abides.**  

The love of Christ has gathered us together,  
Let us rejoice to be united in the Lord,  
Let us revere, let us love the living God.  
With hearts sincere, let us love one another.  

And since we live in unity together,  
Let us take heed that we be not divided,  
Let our disputes, our quarrels and  
contentions cease.  
And so let Jesus, our Lord, in our midst abide.  

And let us also with your Saints,  
O Christ our Lord,  
Behold your face in the glory of heav'n  
Where you, yourself will be  
our happiness and joy,  
Forever more through all ages to come. Amen.
