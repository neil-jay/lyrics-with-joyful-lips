## D.80 My Peace I Leave With You

***Chorus:***  
**My peace I leave with you, my peace I give,**  
**Peace that the world can't bring, I give to you.**  

You must abide in me and I in you,  
For if we live apart, you can't bear fruit.  

Let not your hearts be sad nor be afraid;  
The light is with you now, walk in the light.  

Now if a grain of wheat falls to the earth,  
It will spring up again and bear new life.  

And when the Spirit comes, he'll strengthen you;  
You'll be my witness through all the earth.
