## D.126 Jesus Stand Among Us

Jesus, stand among us at the meeting of our lives,  
Be our sweet agreement at the meeting of our eyes.  
O Jesus, we love You, so we gather here,  
Join our hearts in unity and take away fear.  

So to you we're gathering out of each  
and ev'ry land,  
Christ, the love between us  
at the joining of our hands.  

Jesus, stand among us  
at the breaking of the bread,  
Join us as one body as  
we worship You, our head.
