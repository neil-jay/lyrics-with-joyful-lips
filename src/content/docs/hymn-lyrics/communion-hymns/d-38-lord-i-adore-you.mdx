## D.38 Lord, I Adore You

***Chorus:***  
**Lord, I adore you, who dwells in my heart**  
**And my soul overflows with joy.**  
**Your presence, O Lord, is my greatest delight;**  
**What shall I fear if in me you abide?**  

It is not I who live:  
It is Christ who lives in me.  
If I stay always faithful to his love,  
If I keep in my heart his commandments.  

I give you thanks, O God,  
For the body of the Lord  
That your people together shares in joy.  
May it help us to love one another.  

I give you thanks, O Lord,  
For the gifts on me bestowed,  
For the peace, for the hope and for the help  
That you offer to all those who seek you.  

Let us rejoice in Christ,  
Alleluia, evermore.  
To the end of our life we shall proclaim  
All the blessings of God to his people.
