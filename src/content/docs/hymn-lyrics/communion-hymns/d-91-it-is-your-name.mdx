## D.91 It is Your Name

***Chorus:***  
**It is your name we bear in our hearts**  
**And we know it through Jesus' words of life.**  
**All the love you have given to Him:**  
**In this we share, in this we live.**  

May we all be one in God's own life  
And see his presence so longing to be known.  
And the path that we are free to choose,  
Will lead us on in search of what is real.  

True to his name, we hope to live for ever,  
Seeking peace that comes from deep within.  
When we search for meaning beyond words,  
We'll see a new day dawning in our world.
