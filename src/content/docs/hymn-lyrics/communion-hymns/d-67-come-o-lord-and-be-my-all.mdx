## D.67 Come, o Lord, and be My All

***Chorus:***  
**Come, O Lord, and be my all.**  
**Take my will, my liberty.**  
**Come, O Lord, possess my heart.**  
**Fill it with your charity.**  
**Come, O Lord, and show the path**  
**To eternity.**  

In this world of doubt and darkness,  
Cruel deeds and lack of kindness,  
Stand by ever at my side.  
Yes, Lord, be my guide.  

When my heart is sad and weary,  
When my cross is hard to carry,  
Give me hope, my faith increase.  
Yes, Lord, be my peace.  

I am weak and often stumble,  
But I know you love the humble.  
Help me walk in trust again.  
Yes, Lord, be my friend.
