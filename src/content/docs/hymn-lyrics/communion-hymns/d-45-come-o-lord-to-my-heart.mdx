## D.45 Come, o Lord, to My Heart

***Chorus:***  
**Come, O Lord, to my heart today**  
**And stay with me all the day. (2)**  

Your flesh is food and your blood is drink  
With these you give to me your life.  

This is the bread come down from heaven  
Which, if we eat, we'll live for ever.  

If we take your flesh and blood,  
We will live in you and you in us.  

When you give yourself to us,  
You bind us to yourself and each other.
