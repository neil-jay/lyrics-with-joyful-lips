## D.75 How Good is the Lord

***Chorus:***  
**Oh, how good is the Lord (3)**  
**I never will forget what he has done for me.**  

He gives us his Spirit, how good is the Lord.  

He gives us new life, how good is the Lord.  

He gives us his blessings, how good is the Lord.  

He gives us salvation, how good is the Lord.  

He gives us his healing, how good is the Lord.
