## D 103 do Not Worry: Seek the Kingdom

***Chorus:***  
**Do not worry about your clothing,**  
**your food and drink,**  
**Seek the kingdom and**  
**all the rest will be yours.**  

Think of the ravens: how they neither  
sow nor reap.  
They need to build no barns, no house to store.  
And yet your heavenly Father feeds and  
protects them all.  
Are you not worth much more than birds?  

Think of the lilies of field, see how they grow.  
Can even Solomon with them compare?  
Yet not an anxious thought have they  
to toil or spin.  
Are you not worth much more than flow’rs?  

Provide yourselves with never failing treasures,  
Which thief cannot approach nor moth destroy.  
Sell all that you possess and given in charity.  
For where your treasure is, your heart will be.
