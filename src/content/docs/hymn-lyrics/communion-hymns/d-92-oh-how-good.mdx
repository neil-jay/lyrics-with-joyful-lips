## D.92 Oh, How Good

***Chorus:***  
**O how good, how wonderful it is**  
**When brothers and sisters live as one. (2)**  

It is as fragrant as pure oil,  
The ointment of the Lord,  
Flowing down as on Aaron of old,  
Anointing every one.  

It is as refreshing as the dew  
Upon the mountain of the Lord,  
For there the favour of the Lord remains,  
The blessing of everlasting life.
