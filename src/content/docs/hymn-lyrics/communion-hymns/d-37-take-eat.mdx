## D.37 Take, Eat

***Chorus:***  
**Take, eat, this is my Body.**  
**Take, drink of this cup,**  
**Poured for you and for many.**  
**Do this in remembrance of me.**  

I am the living bread,  
come down from heaven.  
Anyone who eats this bread,  
will live for ever.  

He who eats of this, my flesh,  
and drinks my saving blood,  
Shall abide in me,  
and I abide in him.  

For my flesh is food indeed,  
my blood is really drink.  
Those who eat and drink,  
will have eternal life.
