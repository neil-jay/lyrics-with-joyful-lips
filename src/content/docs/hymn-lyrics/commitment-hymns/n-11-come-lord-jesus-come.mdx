## N.11 Come, Lord Jesus, Come

Come, Lord Jesus, come.  
Come, take my hands, take them for your work.  

***Chorus:***  
**Take them for your service, Lord,**  
**Take them for your glory, Lord.**  
**Come, Lord Jesus, come.**  
**Come, Lord Jesus, take my hands.**  

... eyes, may they shine with joy.  

... lips, may they speak your truth.  

... feet, may they walk your path.  

... heart, fill it with your love.  

... life, take it for your own.
