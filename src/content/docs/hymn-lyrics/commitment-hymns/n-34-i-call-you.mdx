## N.34 I Call You

***Chorus:***  
**I call you from your brothers (sisters),**  
**I send you in my name.**  
**I'll light you with my Spirit's fire**  
**To burn as my love's name.**  
**I call you from your brothers (sisters)**  
**And send you in my name.**  

Tell them what I told to you,  
My words will be your own.  
They'll find me in the news you bring  
As you make my secrets known.  

Bring me all they have and need,  
Because my Father cares.  
Bring back his blessing and his peace  
And the food of life he shares.  

Be a shepherd to my flock  
And love as I have done.  
So they can see what "brother" ("sister") means  
And can feel that they are one.
