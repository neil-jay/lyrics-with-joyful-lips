## N.18 Take My Life

Take my life, O Lord, take my life,  
**That my life be worship.**  
Take my life, O Lord, take my life,  

***Chorus:***  
**That my life resemble your life. (2)**

Take my hands.  

....heart.  

....joy.  

....death.  

Take this bread...  
That this bread become now your flesh.  

Take this wine...  
That this wine be changed to your blood.
