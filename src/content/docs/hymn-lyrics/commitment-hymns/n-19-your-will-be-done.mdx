## N.19 Your Will be Done

***Chorus:***  
**Lord, your will be done,**  
**Yes, Lord, your will be done.**  
**You are our loving Father,**  
**You know what's best for every one.**  

When things are getting out of hand,  
When things don't go on as we planned,  
Then let us put our trust in you  
And let us say:  

Uncertainty is round the bend,  
The worst could substitute the best,  
Our rosy dreams are up in smoke.  
Then let us say:  

When those we loved and those who cared  
Are no more near us — they are dead,  
Our tear filled eyes are blind to see  
That you are there:
