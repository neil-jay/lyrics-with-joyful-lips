## N.38 I Delight Greatly in the Lord

***Chorus:***  
**I delight greatly in the Lord,**  
**My soul rejoices in my God. (2)**  

For He has clothed me with garments of salvation,  
And arrayed me in a robe of righteousness. (2)  

For He has sent me to heal the broken-hearted  
And proclaim to prisoners that they are free. (2)  

For God's own Spirit is poured upon his servant,  
He's anointed me with oil of gladness. (2)
