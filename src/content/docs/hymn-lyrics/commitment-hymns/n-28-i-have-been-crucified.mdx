## N.28 I have been Crucified

***Chorus:***  
**I have been crucified with Christ, (2)**  
**It is no longer I who live**  
**but Christ who lives in me.**  

And the life I now live in the flesh,  
I live by faith in the Son of God  
Who loved me, who loved me  
And gave himself for me.  

Everything I count as a loss because  
Of the precious worth of knowing Christ...  

For in weakness is Jesus' power shown:  
When I'm weak, I know I am strong in Him.
