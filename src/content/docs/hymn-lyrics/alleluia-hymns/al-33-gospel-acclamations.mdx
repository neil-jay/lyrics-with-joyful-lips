## AL.33 Gospel Acclamations

***Chorus:***   
**Alleluia, alleluia!**  

Let us see, Lord, your mercy and give us your help.  

Prepare ye the way, (3)... of the Lord.  

The Spirit of the Lord has been given to me,  
He sends me to give good news to the poor.
