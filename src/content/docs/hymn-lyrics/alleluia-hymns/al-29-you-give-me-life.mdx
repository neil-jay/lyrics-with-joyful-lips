## AL.29 You Give Me Life

***Chorus:***   
**Alleluia (7)**  

You give me life because of your love,  
and I will do your will, yes, I will...  

Train me, Lord, to observe your law,  
to keep it in my heart, to keep it...  

Bend my heart to your will, O Lord,  
and teach me your decrees, yes, teach me...  

Open our hearts, O Lord our God,  
to accept the words of your Son, to accept...
