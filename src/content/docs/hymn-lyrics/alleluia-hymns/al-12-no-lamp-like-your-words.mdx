## AL.12 No Lamp Like Your Words

***Chorus:***   
**There’s no Lamp like your words, Lord,**  
**to guide my steps, Alleluia, alleluia.**  

From the darkness to the light,  
who will be my guide!  

From the desert to green pastures,  
who’ll give me the lead?  

From the gloom of sin and death,  
who will make me free?  

From the tomb to eternal life,  
who will take me safe?
