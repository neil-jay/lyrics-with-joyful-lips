## AL.19 Alleluia

***Chorus:***   
**Alleluia! (5+5)**  

Show me your way, O Lord,  
guide me along your path  
Bend my heart to your will,  
teach me your decrees  

“I am the way”, he says,  
“I am the truth and life,  
None to the father comes,  
but only through me”.
