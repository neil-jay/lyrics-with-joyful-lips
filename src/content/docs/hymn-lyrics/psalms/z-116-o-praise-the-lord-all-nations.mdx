## Z.116 O Praise the Lord, All Nations

***Chorus:***  
**O praise the Lord, all nations,**  
**All peoples, praise his name! (2)**  

His love for us is mighty, his mercy never ends!  

Give glory to the Father, the Son and Spirit blest!  

Laudate omens gentes, laudate Dominum!
