## Z.137 Thanks do I Give to the Lord

***Chorus:***  
**Thanks do I give to the Lord**  
**with my whole heart evermore.**  
**Thanks do I give to the Lord**  
**for his faithfulness and love.**  

In the presence of angels I will bless him  
and adore him before his holy temple.  
On the days I called he answered;  
he increased the strength of my soul.  

For the Lord looks with favour on the humble,  
From afar, he marks down the proud.  
Though I live in the midst of affliction,  
my life he preserves against my foes.  

You stretch out your hand, O Lord, you save me, and  
your right hand will do all things for me.  
For your steadfast love endures for ever.  
Don’t abandon the works your hand has made.
