## Lord I Want to

***Chorus:***  
**Lord I want to, I want to offer you**  
**I want to offer you everything I have**  
**Take my heart Lord, make it like yours, Lord**  
**And make me generous to you Oh Lord. (2)**  

When I get back home and I am on my own  
If I forget this my offering  
Just ring a bell in my heart,  
Make me with a little start  
And I'll remember and I will sing.  

When I come across a splinter of your  
Cross and I feel I cannot bear  
Just give a part on my back,  
Just to tell me you are there and  
I'll take courage and I won't care.  

And in temptation in times of tension  
When I am weak and about to fall  
Just let me lean on your side,  
Let me e'er with you abide  
And in your presence, I'll face it all.
