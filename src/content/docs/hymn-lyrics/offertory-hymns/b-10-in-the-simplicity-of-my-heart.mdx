## B.10 In the Simplicity of My Heart

***Chorus:***  
**O Lord, my God, in the simplicity of my heart,**  
**joyfully I come to give you all,**  
**amidst your people gathered here:**  
**God of Israel, keep us ever faithful.**  

O Lord, you do not ask for sacrifice and offering,  
Then I said: here I am, I am coming!  

In the scroll of the book, am I not commanded  
That I should obey your will, O Lord?  

Of your faithfulness and salvation I shall speak,  
And not hide your love and your truth from the people.
